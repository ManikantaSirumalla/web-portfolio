NewsWave is a dynamic iOS news application designed to keep you updated with the latest news articles and trending news videos. The app offers a seamless user experience with features like Picture-in-Picture (PiP) mode for videos, various news categories, a favorites section, a universal search tab, and a profile tab displaying user details and selected categories. Additionally, NewsWave fetches news based on the user's location for a more personalized experience.

## Features

### Latest News
- Fetches and displays the latest news articles from multiple APIs.
- Keeps you updated with the most recent happenings around the world.

### Trending News Videos
- Integrates trending news videos with a built-in player.
- Supports Picture-in-Picture (PiP) mode for continuous viewing while multitasking.

### News Categories
- Offers a variety of news categories to choose from, ensuring you find content that interests you.

### Favorites
- Allows users to save their favorite articles and videos for easy access later.

### Universal Search
- A comprehensive search feature that lets you find specific articles, videos, and news categories.

### Profile Tab
- Displays user details, selected news categories, and user location.
- Personalizes news content based on the user's location.
