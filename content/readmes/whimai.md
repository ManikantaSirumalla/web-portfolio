WhimAI is an innovative AI-powered chat application that transforms the way users interact with technology. Designed to provide seamless, engaging, and personalized conversations, WhimAI leverages cutting-edge AI technologies and a user-centric design to deliver an unparalleled chat experience.

## Features

### Voice Input and Output
- **Voice Commands**: Interact with the app using your voice. Simply speak, and WhimAI will understand and respond accordingly.
- **Voice Responses**: Get responses read out loud to you in a natural-sounding voice, making interactions more human-like and engaging.

### PDF Export via Email
- **Export Chats**: Easily export your chat histories to a PDF file.
- **Email Integration**: Send the exported PDF directly to your email with just one click, making it simple to save and share important conversations.

### Choose a Voice
- **Voice Selection**: Personalize your chat experience by selecting from five distinct voices: Alex, Samantha, Victoria, Fred, and Karen.
- **Demo Playback**: Listen to a demo sentence in the selected voice to ensure it meets your preference.
- **Waveform Animation**: Enjoy a dynamic wave animation when the app is speaking, and a static linear gradient when it is not, enhancing the visual experience.

### Multi-Image Support
- **Image Sharing**: Send and receive multiple images in a single conversation.
- **Real-Time Updates**: Experience smooth and real-time image sharing within your chats.

## Accessibility

- **Screen Reader Compatibility**: Fully compatible with screen readers to assist visually impaired users.
- **High Contrast Mode**: Enhanced visual options for better readability.
- **Voice Input**: Hands-free operation through voice commands.
- **Voice Output**: Audio feedback for easier understanding and interaction.

## Widgets

- **Small Widget**: Compact and simple, offering quick access to the app.
- **Medium Widget**: Includes the app name and three quick action buttons for starting new chats, viewing recent chats, and accessing settings.
- **Large Widget**: Displays recent chat histories for quick reference and easy access.

## Technologies Used

- **Swift & SwiftUI**: For a smooth and responsive user interface.
- **Combine**: For managing asynchronous events and data binding.
- **UIKit**: For handling more complex UI elements.
- **AVFoundation**: For voice input and output capabilities.
- **Firebase**: For authentication and real-time database management.
- **MailGun**: For seamless email integration.
- **MessageUI**: For composing and sending emails from within the app.
